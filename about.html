Atelier Heritage

MACHINES. CRAFT. OBSESSION.

We are a collective of mechanical engineers, certified aviation welders, and obsessive vintage restorers dedicated to the art of the bespoke motorcycle.

Our Origin

HOW A DRAFTY BRICK SHED BECAME A WORLD ATELIER

Apex & Iron Co. began in 2012 in an industrial alley with a borrowed English wheel, a Miller TIG torch, and a barn-find 1972 CB500. Founder and aircraft airframe specialist Liam Vance refused to accept the disposable mindset spreading through contemporary motorcycling.

Over the following decade, what began as late-night passion builds turned into an internationally commissioned workshop. We invested relentlessly in chassis jigs, engine clean rooms, and dyno diagnostic facilities while strictly preserving traditional metal-shaping hand skills.

14+ Years of Innovation

Guiding Principles

THE FIVE CORNERSTONES OF OUR CRAFT

Every decision made in our bays is governed by five uncompromising standards.

Quality Without Compromise

We do not cut corners behind cosmetic bodywork. Invisible welds, internal bracket gussets, and wire routings receive the exact same scrutiny as outer paint finishes.

Built With Purpose

We refuse to build fragile "trailer queens." Every motorcycle leaving our workshop is engineered with real-world rake, trail, oil flow, and suspension travel to be ridden hard.

Attention to Microns

From valve lash tolerances to triple clamp offsets and seat pan seam stitching, our builders obsess over details imperceptible to casual observers.

Transparent Communication

You receive direct access to your build ledger, milestone schedules, and weekly 4K workshop photography. No hidden surcharges, no mysterious delays.

Uncompromising Rider Safety

Braking swept area, structural shear strength, tire load indices, and DOT compliance take precedence over transient aesthetic gimmicks.

Rider Individuality

We never stamp cookie-cutter kits. Every frame loop, handlebar pull-back, footpeg sweep, and fuel capacity is calibrated to your physical measurements.
