Vintage Motorcycle Restoration

Careful mechanical and cosmetic restoration that respects the motorcycle’s history and original details.

Vintage Motorcycle Restoration at Apex & Iron

Every restoration starts by understanding what is original, what has changed and what the machine needs to become reliable again.

We document the motorcycle before strip-down, inspect the frame and major assemblies, and build a parts plan around your preservation goals. Work can prioritize factory-correct detail, dependable road use or a considered balance of both.

Who this service is for

Owners preserving a family motorcycle, returning a long-stored classic to the road or documenting a period-correct restoration.

What this service includes

  • Condition report and photo record
  • Strip-down and component inspection
  • Frame and corrosion assessment
  • Parts research and sourcing
  • Mechanical and electrical restoration
  • Paint, reassembly and road test

Our workshop process

  1. 01Initial assessment
  2. 02Documentation and teardown
  3. 03Parts and finish plan
  4. 04Restoration by assembly
  5. 05Careful reassembly
  6. 06Road test and handover

Workshop tools and equipment

  • Engine measuring tools
  • Frame alignment fixtures
  • Parts cleaning equipment
  • Period-correct reference material

Why choose this service

  • Original details are recorded before work
  • Parts choices follow your preservation brief
  • Hidden wear is addressed before final assembly

Timing and planning

The schedule depends on the motorcycle’s condition and the availability of correct replacement parts.
