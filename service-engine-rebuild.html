Engine Rebuilding

Engine teardown, inspection and rebuilding planned around measured wear, parts condition and your riding needs.

Engine Rebuilding at Apex & Iron

A dependable rebuild starts with measurements and inspection, not a parts list chosen before the engine is opened.

We document the engine as it comes apart, inspect critical surfaces and clearances, and identify which components can be reused, machined or replaced. The assembly plan is reviewed before parts are ordered and final tolerances are set.

Who this service is for

Owners facing wear, low compression, oil consumption or an engine that needs a documented return to service.

What this service includes

  • Compression and leak-down checks
  • Engine teardown and cleaning
  • Cylinder, crank and bearing inspection
  • Valve, seal and gasket work
  • Measured reassembly
  • Initial startup and break-in guidance

Our workshop process

  1. 01Baseline checks
  2. 02Teardown and inspection
  3. 03Machine work and parts plan
  4. 04Measured assembly
  5. 05Startup checks
  6. 06Break-in handover

Workshop tools and equipment

  • Torque and angle tools
  • Micrometers and bore gauges
  • Engine stands and fixtures
  • Valve service tools

Why choose this service

  • Wear is documented before decisions
  • Machining follows measured requirements
  • Assembly settings are recorded for service

Lead time depends on machine-shop work and the availability of correct engine parts.
