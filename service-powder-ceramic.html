Powder & Ceramic Coating

Protective coating options for frames, wheels and exhaust components, chosen for substrate, heat and intended use.

Powder & Ceramic Coating at Apex & Iron

Powder and ceramic coatings serve different purposes, so the right finish depends on the part, substrate and operating temperature.

We identify the component material and service conditions, then recommend a suitable preparation and coating route. Parts are stripped and prepared to the coating specification, with masking and fit surfaces protected before final inspection.

Who this service is for

Owners preparing removable frame, wheel or exhaust parts for a protective finish matched to material and operating conditions.

What this service includes

  • Part and substrate assessment
  • Coating selection by use
  • Surface preparation
  • Frame, wheel and bracket coating
  • Heat-resistant exhaust coating options
  • Masking and fit-surface checks

Our workshop process

  1. 01Inspect and identify substrate
  2. 02Choose a suitable coating
  3. 03Strip and prepare the part
  4. 04Mask critical interfaces
  5. 05Apply and cure coating
  6. 06Inspect coverage and fit

Workshop tools and equipment

  • Media preparation equipment
  • Masking and coating tools
  • Cure equipment for compatible parts
  • Surface inspection tools

Why choose this service

  • Coating choice reflects heat and exposure
  • Preparation supports adhesion and coverage
  • Critical fit surfaces are considered before coating

Timing and planning

Timing depends on stripping, preparation, coating batches and cure requirements.
