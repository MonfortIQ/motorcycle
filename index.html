Systematic Methodology

THE SIX-STAGE BUILD PROCESS

Transparency from raw chassis to tarmac. Every project follows our rigorous engineering milestone protocol.

01

Tell Us Your Vision

We conduct an in-depth ergonomics consultation, donor bike inspection, budget mapping, and riding style assessment.

02

Design & 3D Planning

CAD modeling, stance sketches, component sourcing list, and structural load calculations for bespoke frame lines.

03

Strip & Fabricate

Complete tear-down to bare frame. Hand metalwork, custom subframe fabrication, tank shaping, and bracket welding.

04

Mechanical & Wiring

Precision engine rebuilding, suspension setup, brake lines, and bespoke m-Unit digital wiring harness loom installation.

05

Paint & Finishing

Multi-stage paint booth application, hand-laid leafing, frame powder coating, engine vapor blast, and hand upholstery.

06

Road Test & Handover

Dyno tuning, 500 km shake-down road testing, comprehensive photo documentation book, and nationwide delivery.

The Atelier Standard

WHY COMMISSION WITH APEX & IRON

We treat every motorcycle not as a transaction, but as a signature testament to our engineering pedigree.

100% Built In-House

No passing your project between random subcontractors. Every single cut, weld, wire, and paint layer happens under our own roof.

Master Fabricators

Our builders come from aircraft fabrication, Isle of Man TT race prep, and museum-grade automotive preservation backgrounds.

Rider-Focused Design

Show bikes that can't be ridden are useless. We engineer ergonomics, rake, trail, and ride height directly around your anatomy.

Weekly Build Updates

Clear weekly 4K photo updates, video breakdowns, and transparent milestone tracking throughout your motorcycle project.

Tier-One Hardware Only

We partner exclusively with Brembo, Öhlins, Motogadget, Koso, Excel, and Mikuni to ensure race-grade reliability.

Road-Tested & Certified

Before keys exchange hands, every motorcycle completes our strict 500-kilometer thermal, electrical, and handling shakedown.
