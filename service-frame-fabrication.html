Motorcycle Frame Fabrication

Frame repairs, subframes and chassis changes planned around alignment, clearance and the motorcycle’s intended use.

Motorcycle Frame Fabrication at Apex & Iron

Frame work affects how a motorcycle fits together and behaves, so measurement and fixture setup come before cutting or welding.

We inspect the existing structure, record reference points and discuss the intended geometry. Changes are laid out and held in a suitable jig, then checked for alignment and component clearance before the frame moves on to finishing.

Who this service is for

Projects requiring a repaired chassis, a custom subframe or a carefully measured change to frame mounting and geometry.

What this service includes

  • Frame and weld inspection
  • Reference measurements
  • Subframe and loop fabrication
  • Mounts and reinforcement
  • Jig setup and alignment checks
  • Trial assembly and final inspection

Our workshop process

  1. 01Inspect the chassis
  2. 02Record geometry
  3. 03Plan modifications
  4. 04Fixture and fabricate
  5. 05Check alignment
  6. 06Trial fit components

Workshop tools and equipment

  • Chassis jig
  • Alignment and measurement tools
  • TIG welding equipment
  • Fixture and clamping systems

Why choose this service

  • Geometry is documented before changes
  • Fixtures reduce movement during welding
  • Clearance is checked with related components

Timing and planning

Frame projects require inspection, planning and fixture availability; timing is confirmed after measurements.
