Brake Upgrades

Brake inspection and component upgrades selected for control, fitment and the motorcycle’s intended use.

Brake Upgrades at Apex & Iron

Brake performance depends on the whole system: lever feel, hydraulics, pad compound, rotor condition, tires and setup.

We inspect the current system and identify the limits or faults before recommending a change. Calipers, master cylinders, lines, rotors and pads are checked for compatibility, then installed, bled and tested as a complete package.

Who this service is for

Owners who need improved brake control or replacement components selected to work together on their motorcycle.

What this service includes

  • System condition assessment
  • Caliper and master cylinder review
  • Pad and rotor selection
  • Brake line inspection or upgrade
  • Fluid service and bleeding
  • Static and road function checks

Our workshop process

  1. 01Inspect components and symptoms
  2. 02Confirm fitment and use
  3. 03Select compatible parts
  4. 04Install and torque components
  5. 05Bleed and verify lever feel
  6. 06Road test and recheck

Workshop tools and equipment

  • Brake bleeding equipment
  • Torque tools
  • Rotor and pad inspection tools
  • Hydraulic service tools

Why choose this service

  • Parts are matched as a working system
  • Worn or incompatible components are found early
  • Installation is followed by function checks

Timing and planning

A component upgrade can often be scheduled in a workshop visit once parts and compatibility are confirmed.
