Custom Motorcycle Builds at Apex & Iron

A custom motorcycle begins with a clear brief: how it should look, where it will be ridden and what the finished machine must do.

We inspect the donor, establish a practical design direction and plan the build as one connected system. Frame, controls, engine, electrics and finish are coordinated before fabrication begins, with milestone reviews keeping the work aligned to the agreed scope.

Who this service is for

Riders with a donor motorcycle and a clear idea of the machine they want to ride, whether that means a daily custom, scrambler or weekend build.

What this service includes

  • Concept and design direction
  • Donor motorcycle inspection
  • Rider fit and component selection
  • Frame and bodywork fabrication
  • Mechanical work and wiring
  • Finishing, assembly and road test

  • Documented milestones from teardown to handover
