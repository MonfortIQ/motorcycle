Suspension Setup

Suspension inspection and adjustment tailored to rider weight, road use, luggage and the motorcycle’s components.

Suspension Setup at Apex & Iron

Good suspension setup starts with a motorcycle in sound condition and a clear understanding of how it is ridden.

We check the basic condition of forks, shocks, bearings and tires, then measure sag and adjust the available settings. Spring changes or internal service are recommended only when adjustment cannot address the mismatch.

Who this service is for

Riders who want their current suspension adjusted around their weight, roads, luggage and riding pace.

What this service includes

  • Suspension condition review
  • Rider sag measurement
  • Preload and damping adjustment
  • Fork and shock evaluation
  • Spring-rate recommendations
  • Road validation and notes

Our workshop process

  1. 01Discuss use and rider load
  2. 02Inspect chassis and suspension
  3. 03Measure baseline sag
  4. 04Adjust available settings
  5. 05Evaluate response
  6. 06Road test and record setup

Workshop tools and equipment

  • Sag measurement tools
  • Fork service tools
  • Shock setup equipment
  • Torque tools and setup notes

Why choose this service

  • Settings match the rider and intended use
  • Mechanical issues are identified before tuning
  • Baseline measurements make later changes comparable

Timing and planning

A setup session can often be completed in one visit; parts replacement or internal service needs additional scheduling.
