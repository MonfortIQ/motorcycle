Paint and Finish

Custom Paint & Finishing

Custom color and finish work for tanks, fenders and motorcycle components, from surface preparation to final polish.

Custom Paint & Finishing at Apex & Iron

A durable finish is built through careful preparation and a clear agreement on color, texture and detailing.

We assess the existing surface, discuss paint direction and confirm the finish system before preparation starts. Panels are repaired and prepared for the selected coating, with graphics or pinstriping planned into the sequence.

Who this service is for

Riders with a defined color idea, a damaged panel or a full build that needs a considered paint and graphics package.

What this service includes

  • Color and finish consultation
  • Panel condition assessment
  • Surface preparation
  • Tank and fender paintwork
  • Graphics or pinstriping coordination
  • Clear coat and final inspection

Our workshop process

  1. 01Choose color and finish
  2. 02Remove and inspect panels
  3. 03Prepare and repair surfaces
  4. 04Apply color and detailing
  5. 05Cure and finish coat
  6. 06Polish and inspect

Workshop tools and equipment

  • Paint preparation tools
  • Spray equipment and booth
  • Color matching references
  • Polishing and inspection lights

Why choose this service

  • Finish expectations are agreed before painting
  • Preparation addresses surface condition first
  • Graphics and clear coat are planned together

Timing and planning

Paint schedules include preparation, coating and cure time; timing depends on finish complexity and panel condition.
