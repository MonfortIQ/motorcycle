Privacy Policy

How Apex & Iron handles information submitted through the website.

Information you submit

When you use a contact or newsletter form, the information you enter is sent to Apex & Iron so the workshop can respond to your request or send the updates you asked to receive. Form submissions are processed through FormSubmit.

Website preferences

The site saves your selected color theme and text direction in your browser so those display preferences can be remembered on later visits.

Questions or requests

For questions about a form submission or workshop updates, email builds@apexandiron.com. Include the email address used in your submission so we can identify it.
