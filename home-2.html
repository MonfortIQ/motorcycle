The Atelier Ethos

THE REVERENCE OF RAW STEEL & OIL

A vintage motorcycle is not an appliance. It carries mechanical history in the grain of its sand-cast aluminum crankcase and the pulse of its carbureted idle.

When we take on a restoration or bespoke fabrication commission, we don't erase the patina of character. Instead, we elevate it. We correct the metallurgical weaknesses of 1970s metallurgy with modern heat-treating, optimize frame stiffness for contemporary radial tires, and wire the entire bike through invisible mil-spec looms.

Historical Accuracy

We preserve period-correct thread pitches, cadmium plating, and factory color formulas when requested.

Modern Reliability

Electronic ignition, solid-state regulators, and braided stainless brake lines hidden within classic architecture.

Six Restoration Disciplines

RESTORATION EXPERTISE

From museum preservation to high-compression restomods, our specialist bays handle every mechanical requirement.

Classic Restoration

Period-accurate nut-and-bolt teardowns honoring original factory specifications, OEM fasteners, and historical finishes.

  • OEM Cadmium & Zinc Plating
  • Factory Decal Restoration

Engine Restoration

Boring, honing, crack testing, valve seat cutting, balanced crankshafts, and ultra-fine glass bead / vapor blasting.

  • Vapor Honed Cases
  • High-Torque Cam Profiles

Frame Restoration

Laser alignment verification on our chassis jig, rust de-scaling, reinforcing gussets, and high-impact powder coating.

  • Frame Straightening Jigs
  • De-Tabbed Custom Loops

Electrical Restoration

Replacing brittle 40-year-old wiring harnesses with Motogadget Bluetooth controllers, LED conversions, and reliable stators.

  • Modern Mosfet Regulators
  • Electronic CDI Conversion

Paint & Tank Restoration

Chemical tank de-rusting, Caswell internal tank epoxy liner sealing, metallic base coats, and traditional gold leaf pinstriping.

  • Original Color Formulation
  • Fuel-Resistant High-Gloss

Global Parts Sourcing

Direct dealer connections in Japan, Italy, Germany, and the UK to locate rare NOS (New Old Stock) parts and authentic castings.

  • Rare NOS Inventory
  • Custom CNC Recreation
Concours Case Study

1978 HONDA CB750 FOUR:
THE RESURRECTION OF AN ICON

Found decaying in an unheated barn in upstate New York after 31 years of slumber. Rodents had stripped the wiring loom, moisture had pitted the cylinder walls, and the carburetors were completely calcified.

Time Invested 380 Workshop Hours
Donor Condition Seized & Barn Rusted
Displacement 836cc Wiseco Big Bore
Final Dyno Output 84.2 BHP @ 8,500 RPM
Key Work Performed:
  • Full engine teardown, vapor blasting, 836cc forged piston kit, high-lift camshaft.
  • Keihin CR29 smooth-bore carburetors with billet velocity stacks.
  • Handcrafted 4-into-1 brushed stainless steel exhaust header.
  • Hand-shaped aluminum cafe racer seat cowl with Connolly leather saddle.
