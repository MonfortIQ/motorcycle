TIG Welding

Precision TIG welding for motorcycle steel, stainless and aluminium components, repairs and custom assemblies.

TIG Welding at Apex & Iron

TIG welding is suited to controlled, clean joints where access, heat input and finish matter.

Before welding, we identify the base material, joint preparation and expected loads. We set up the work to maintain alignment, select an appropriate filler and process, then inspect and finish the joint to suit its location and purpose.

Who this service is for

Owners and builders with a sound part that needs a precise repair, a controlled joint or a cleanly fabricated assembly.

What this service includes

  • Material and joint assessment
  • Stainless and mild steel work
  • Aluminium welding where suitable
  • Exhaust and bracket joins
  • Repair and reinforcement work
  • Weld inspection and cleanup

Our workshop process

  1. 01Identify material
  2. 02Prepare and fixture parts
  3. 03Set process and filler
  4. 04Weld with controlled heat
  5. 05Inspect the joint
  6. 06Finish and verify fit

Workshop tools and equipment

  • TIG power source
  • Purge equipment for suitable tubing
  • Welding fixtures and tables
  • Material preparation tools

Why choose this service

  • Process selected for the base material
  • Fixtures help protect alignment
  • Weld finish is considered alongside service loads

Timing and planning

Timing varies with access, preparation, material condition and whether parts need to be removed from the motorcycle.
