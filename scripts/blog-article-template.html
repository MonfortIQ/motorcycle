Build Planning & Methodology

HOW WE PLAN A FULL CUSTOM MOTORCYCLE BUILD

Liam Vance (Lead Builder) October 12, 2026 7 min read 14 Comments

Every memorable custom motorcycle begins long before an angle grinder touches a frame loop. The difference between a cohesive, road-hugging machine and an awkward collection of catalog parts lies completely in the planning phase.

STAGE 1: THE ERGONOMIC TRIANGLE & RIDING SILHOUETTE

The fundamental geometry of motorcycling revolves around the Ergonomic Triangle: the spatial relationship between the seat contact patch, the footpeg position, and the handlebar grip clamp centers.

When a client commissions a cafe racer, the temptation is often to slap low clip-ons directly onto the fork tubes without adjusting foot controls. The result is severe lower back pain and cramping. In our workshop, we mount the donor bike on our adjustable laser alignment jig and seat the rider directly on dummy blocks to measure knee angle, torso inclination, and wrist weight distribution.

"A custom motorcycle that hurts to ride after 20 minutes is not a triumph of design; it is an engineering failure. True craftsmanship harmonizes aggressive visual lines with human musculoskeletal biomechanics."

STAGE 2: CHASSIS RAKE, TRAIL & STANCE

Lowering the front end to achieve that aggressive cafe rake looks stunning in stationary studio lighting, but altering fork length directly changes trail. If trail drops below 85mm, the motorcycle becomes twitchy and prone to high-speed headshake (tank slappers). If trail exceeds 115mm, steering becomes sluggish and resistant to turn-in.

STAGE 3: THE BREADBOARD WIRE LOOM & ECU PACKAGING

Vintage Japanese and British electrical systems are notorious for brittle wire insulation, mechanical flasher relays, and massive lead-acid battery trays. During initial teardown, we discard the entire harness in favor of the Motogadget m-Unit Blue solid-state digital controller.

By running ultra-thin push-button control wires inside the handlebars and through custom frame channels, we eliminate messy wire bundles while gaining Bluetooth keyless starting, automated alarm sensors, and customizable hazard modes.

Written by Liam Vance
Founder & Master Chassis Fabricator

Liam has engineered over 180 bespoke motorcycles and raced vintage Isle of Man machines across Europe for more than a decade.
