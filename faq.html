FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about donor platform selection, build milestones, metal fabrication lead times, and road certification.

1. TIMELINES & PLANNING

A complete bespoke ground-up build takes between 14 to 22 weeks. A classic nut-and-bolt restoration takes 16 to 26 weeks, depending on rare NOS parts sourcing. Partial upgrades (such as an exhaust header or front-end swap) typically take 3 to 6 weeks.

Yes, this is our specialty. Barn finds that have sat for 20 to 40 years are completely disassembled down to bare crankcases. We chemically descale and vapor-blast all castings, re-sleeve or bore cylinders, cut new multi-angle valve seats, and replace every seal, bearing, and fastener.

Every commissioned client receives weekly high-resolution photo logs and video walkthroughs directly from Liam and Mateo. You are invited to follow every welding pass, paint coat, and dyno pull.

2. DONOR BIKES & FABRICATION
